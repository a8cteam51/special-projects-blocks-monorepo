## 0.1.3 (2026-10-27)

### Fixed

-   Reaction buttons are announced by their label and count (e.g. "Heart 3") instead of the reaction's slug. When labels are hidden, the label is still in the button for screen readers.
-   Changing a reaction while logged in attributes it to that user, so a reaction first made as a guest in the same browser no longer stays a guest reaction.
